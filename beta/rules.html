社区规则

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日

你可以把自己拍的照片公开到城市相册，让更多人看到那里的风光、活动和文化。公开到城市相册的内容请遵守以下规则：

欢迎分享

  • 你自己拍摄的、与这座城市有关的风光、活动和文化照片或短视频；
  • 真实、友善的照片说明。

请勿发布

  • 违反法律法规的内容，危害国家安全、破坏民族团结、宣扬恐怖主义或极端主义的内容；
  • 色情、低俗、赌博、毒品相关内容；
  • 暴力、血腥、虐待动物等令人不适的内容；
  • 侮辱、歧视、骚扰、人身攻击他人；
  • 泄露他人隐私，例如未经同意发布他人的清晰面容、证件、车牌、住址等；
  • 盗用他人作品，或冒充他人；
  • 广告、刷屏、引流和诈骗信息；
  • 谣言和故意误导他人的信息。

举报与处理

看到不合适的内容，请点“举报”。同一内容被多人举报后会先自动隐藏，等待处理。我们会根据情况恢复、删除内容，或限制、封禁账号。如果你对处理结果有异议，可以发邮件至 moslmos@163.com。

Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 18, 2026

You can share your photos with a city’s album so others can see its scenery, activities and culture. Anything you share there must follow these guidelines:

Please share

  • Photos and short videos you took that show the city’s scenery, activities or culture;
  • Honest, friendly captions.

Don’t post

  • Anything illegal, or content promoting terrorism or extremism;
  • Sexual content, gambling or drugs;
  • Violence, gore, animal cruelty or other disturbing content;
  • Insults, discrimination, harassment or personal attacks;
  • Other people’s private information — such as identifiable faces without consent, IDs, license plates or addresses;
  • Other people’s work, or impersonation;
  • Ads, spam, scams or traffic farming;
  • Rumors or deliberately misleading information.

Reports

If you see something that doesn’t belong, tap Report. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically until it’s reviewed. We may restore or remove content and restrict or ban accounts. To appeal, email moslmos@163.com.
