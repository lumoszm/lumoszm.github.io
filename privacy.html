隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日

ATLAS（以下称“我们”）是由个人运营的旅行地图网站：你可以建立只属于自己的地图册，也可以浏览各个城市的风光、活动和文化照片。我们尽量少收集信息，只用于让网站正常工作。本政策说明我们收集什么、为什么收集、存在哪里，以及你可以怎样管理自己的信息。

一、我们收集的信息

  • 账号信息：你的邮箱地址（用于发送登录验证码和必要的账号通知），以及你自己填写的昵称、头像和简介。
  • 你上传的内容：照片、短视频及其说明文字，你为它们选择的城市、分类，以及拍摄日期。
  • 举报信息：你提交的举报及理由。
  • 旅行足迹（敏感个人信息）：在你单独同意后，我们会根据你上传内容所选的城市、拍摄日期以及你手动添加的地方生成足迹地图。足迹地图只有你自己能看到。
  • 技术信息：为保障安全，云服务商会记录必要的访问日志（例如 IP 地址、访问时间、浏览器类型）。

二、关于照片里的位置信息

手机拍摄的照片和视频常常带有精确的 GPS 坐标。上传时，网页会在你自己的设备上读取拍摄地点，仅用于推荐所属城市；随后照片会被重新压缩，所有元数据（包括 GPS、相机型号等）都会被去除，视频文件里的位置信息也会被清零。我们只保存你选择的城市，不保存精确坐标。

三、我们如何使用这些信息

  • 提供登录、个人地图册、足迹地图和城市相册等功能；
  • 处理举报，维护社区秩序，防止滥用；
  • 保障网站安全和正常运行。

我们不会出售你的个人信息，不做个性化广告，也不会把你的信息用于与上述目的无关的用途。

四、谁能看到你的内容

  • 你的地图册、足迹地图和简介只有你自己能看到。上传时默认“只存到我的地图册”。
  • 只有你主动选择“也公开到城市相册”的照片和视频，才会出现在对应城市的相册里，所有访问者都能看到，同时显示你的昵称和头像。你可以随时取消公开或删除。

五、存储地点与跨境

网站页面托管在 GitHub Pages，精选照片由 Unsplash 的图片服务器提供；账号、数据库和文件存储使用 Supabase 提供的云服务，服务器位于新加坡。如果你在中国大陆使用本网站，你的个人信息会被传输到境外存储和处理。注册时我们会就此单独征得你的同意。上述服务商仅按我们的指示为网站提供基础设施服务。

六、保存期限

你的信息在账号存续期间保存。你删除某项内容后，它会立即从网站上移除（已公开过的图片地址可能因网络缓存在一小时内仍能打开）；注销账号后，你的资料、照片、视频和足迹会被永久删除。服务商的安全日志和备份会在其保留周期结束后自动清除。

七、你的权利

  • 随时查看、修改你的资料，删除你上传的任何内容；
  • 随时把照片取消公开或重新公开；
  • 撤回对足迹记录的同意（在“编辑资料”中关闭），撤回后你手动添加的足迹会被删除；
  • 在“编辑资料”中注销账号；
  • 通过下方联系方式就个人信息问题与我们联系，我们会在 15 个工作日内答复。

八、未成年人

本网站面向成年人。未满 14 周岁的未成年人请勿注册和上传内容；14 至 18 周岁的用户请在监护人同意和指导下使用。

九、本地存储

网站使用浏览器的本地存储保存你的登录状态和语言设置，不使用第三方追踪或广告 Cookie。

十、政策更新

政策如有重要变化，我们会在网站上提示。继续使用即表示你接受更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

ATLAS (“we”) is a travel map site run by an individual: you can keep a private map album of your own trips and browse photos of cities’ scenery, activities and culture. We collect as little as possible and only to make the site work. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is stored and how you can manage it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address (to send sign-in codes and essential account notices) and the name, photo and bio you choose to add.
  • Your uploads: photos, short videos, captions, the city and category you choose for them and the date they were taken.
  • Reports: reports you submit and their reasons.
  • Travel map (sensitive): only with your separate consent, we build a map from the cities and dates of your uploads and places you add by hand. Only you can see it.
  • Technical data: our cloud providers keep basic access logs (such as IP address, time and browser type) for security.

Location data in your photos

Photos and videos often contain exact GPS coordinates. When you upload, the page reads the location on your own device only to suggest a city. The photo is then re-encoded and all metadata, including GPS and camera details, is removed; location data inside videos is zeroed out. We store only the city you choose, never exact coordinates.

How we use it

  • To provide sign-in, your map album, your travel map and city albums;
  • To handle reports, keep the community safe and prevent abuse;
  • To keep the site secure and running.

We don’t sell your data, don’t run personalized ads and don’t use your data for anything else.

Who can see what

  • Your map album, travel map and bio are visible only to you. Uploads are kept in your own album by default.
  • Only items you choose to “Also share to city albums” appear in that city’s album, visible to everyone together with your name and profile photo. You can stop sharing or delete them at any time.

Where data is stored

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; curated photos are served by Unsplash. Accounts, the database and file storage run on Supabase, with servers in Singapore. If you are outside Singapore, including in mainland China, your data is transferred abroad; we ask for your separate consent when you sign up. These providers only supply infrastructure for the site.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleted items are removed from the site immediately (a previously public image address may keep working for up to an hour because of network caching). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photos, videos and travel map. Providers’ security logs and backups expire on their own schedules.

Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your profile and delete anything you uploaded;
  • Stop sharing items or share them again;
  • Withdraw consent for the travel map in Edit profile — places you added by hand are then deleted;
  • Delete your account in Edit profile;
  • Contact us about your data; we reply within 15 working days.

Children

ATLAS is meant for adults. Children under 14 must not sign up; users aged 14–18 should use the site with a parent or guardian’s consent.

Local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember your language. We don’t use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes

We’ll post a notice on the site about significant changes.
